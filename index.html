About Me

A life shaped by faith, family, and the classroom.

I am a Christian wife and mother whose calling has taken me from elementary classrooms in Indonesia to the mountains of Washington State. For over a decade I have taught, mentored, directed, and coached — always with the conviction that every child deserves to be seen, challenged, and encouraged.

My background spans International Baccalaureate curriculum, Biblical Studies, drama direction, fitness leadership, and substitute teaching across every grade level. I bring the same energy, warmth, and professionalism to a kindergarten circle time as I do to a high school drama rehearsal.

Currently pursuing my Washington State Teaching Certificate, I am excited to step into a full-time classroom role where I can continue building relationships and cultivating a love of learning.
